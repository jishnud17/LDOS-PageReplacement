CONSISTENT WINNERS (reactive everywhere, survive both method and workload)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

random_walk_index_high  (trend-vs-noise detector)
  P1:   #1 signal in all 3 workloads, both event types (scores 1.1 - 7.0)
  CTL:  top-5 both events (1.12 - 1.47), consistency up to 1.00
  GUPS: consistency 1.00 on BOTH event types (7.3 sigma on cold_to_hot)
  VERDICT: the most robust signal in the study.  Primary phase-2 feature.

swing_index / accum_swing_index  (per-bar move size / its cumulative sum)
  P1:   top-3 alongside rwi_high everywhere
  CTL:  swing_index #1 on cold_to_hot (2.20 sigma); asi at consistency 1.00
  GUPS: 1.0 - 7.0 sigma, consistency 0.83 - 0.94
  VERDICT: confirmed.  Redundant with each other -- keep ONE (swing_index).

random_walk_index_low  (short-window trend)
  P1:   consistently present, ~60-80% of rwi_high's score
  CTL:  #1 on hot_to_cold (1.64), consistency 0.96
  GUPS: 0.93 consistency
  VERDICT: promoted.  The short window catches demotions the long window
  smooths over; keep BOTH rwi variants.

range_action_verification_index  (short-vs-long MA momentum)
  P1:   mid-field
  CTL:  #2 on hot_to_cold (1.57) at 0.96 consistency
  GUPS: consistency 1.00 on hot_to_cold
  VERDICT: promoted from phase-1's "optional" to candidate.

parabolic_sar / parabolic_sar_direction  (trend-flip level / flag)
  P1:   psar_dir a reliable coincident direction flag (0.24 - 1.11)
  CTL:  both ~0.75 - 1.00, coincident
  GUPS: psar 4.99 on hot_to_cold (psar_dir noisy at coarse cadence)
  VERDICT: confirmed as the cheap direction feature.

CONFIRMED ROLE PLAYER (reacts, but always late)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

schaff_trend_cycle  (cycle detector)
  P1:   consistent (0.74 - 0.93) but lags +50 to +100ms in all 3 workloads
  CTL:  identical behavior: 0.95 consistency, +50 to +150ms lag
  GUPS: collapses at coarse cadence (0.41 consistency) -- it needs multiple
        bars to confirm, and coarse bars deny it that
  VERDICT: keep as a CONFIRMATION feature only.  Never predictive.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD-DEPENDENT (the two most important nuanced findings)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

inter_access_interval_ms  (mean time gap between accesses)
  P1:   the ONLY signal that LED transitions: cold_to_hot by 100-250ms,
        scores up to 3.2 (temporal workload).  The headline phase-1 finding.
  CTL:  dead (0.01).   GUPS: dead (0.001).
  WHY:  not a workload effect -- a measurement-granularity effect.  Phase-1's
        userfaultfd faults timestamped each access individually; telemetry
        mode quantizes access times to the 10ms PEBS merge cadence, which
        destroys the gap structure the signal measures.
  VERDICT: the predictive lead is real but CONDITIONAL on per-access
  timestamps.  Becomes a phase-2 engineering requirement (timestamp PEBS
  samples at ingest -- perf records carry timestamps; we currently discard
  them at merge), not a ready-made feature.

system_quality_number  (mean/std of rate changes)
  P1:   top-3 with rwi_high/swing_index (up to 5.5 sigma, 0.96-1.00 consist)
  CTL:  collapsed to 0.04.   GUPS: amplitude present but 0.24 consistency.
  WHY:  PEBS-estimated rates move in sampling-period-sized quanta (x5003);
        the quantization inflates SQN's std denominator.
  VERDICT: artifact-sensitive.  DROP from the shortlist.  Also a caution:
  phase-1's sqn/rwi/si "top trio" was partially an artifact of the smoother
  UFFD-derived rate series.

================================================================================
FINAL CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. The core signal hierarchy is real.  The random-walk / swing-index
   volatility family tops every dataset where the 50ms cadence held --
   across two collection methods and on a real benchmark's transitions
   (rwi_high: 1.00 consistency on GUPS events).

2. The predictive-lead finding survives, with a condition attached.
   inter_access_interval_ms leads transitions only when accesses carry
   individual timestamps.  This converts a phase-1 observation into a
   concrete phase-2 design requirement rather than invalidating it.

3. Two phase-1 signals were exposed as measurement artifacts (SQN
   materially; VHF/GAPO likely) by changing the collection method --
   exactly the kind of error the control run existed to catch.

4. Platform requirements are now characterized: Haswell-class PEBS
   attributes stores only (loads report garbage addresses -- confirmed
   with stock perf tooling); full load+store telemetry needs Skylake+.
   Signal discrimination additionally requires holding the 50ms cadence,
   which requires bounding tracked pages (~20-50K max on current code).

5. Remaining validation gaps, in priority order: (a) bounded tracking ->
   clean GUPS rankings; (b) Skylake node -> PageRank/Twitter, the
   read-dominant workload class; (c) replication runs.
