SIGNAL GLOSSARY (what each of the 19 signals measures)
================================================================================

 1. RANDOM WALK INDEX (rwi_high/rwi_low): how far the access rate actually
    traveled over the window vs. how far a random walk would travel.  High =
    directional movement, not jitter.  Long- and short-window variants.
 2. SWING INDEX (swing_index): how decisive the current bar's move is --
    bar-to-bar rate change scaled by the bar's range.
 3. ACCUMULATIVE SWING INDEX (accum_swing_index): running cumulative sum of
    the swing index -- move size with memory.
 4. RANGE ACTION VERIFICATION INDEX (RAVI): percent gap between a short and
    a long moving average of the rate -- momentum vs. established trend.
 5. PARABOLIC SAR (parabolic_sar/_direction): trailing level that chases the
    rate and accelerates as a trend extends; direction = +1/-1 side flag.
 6. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE (schaff_trend_cycle/_signal): cycle detector on
    double-smoothed moving averages; 0-100, extremes = trend established.
    Needs several bars of evidence -- structurally a lagging confirmer.
 7. AVERAGE DIRECTIONAL INDEX (ADX): direction-free trend strength --
    smoothed, normalized difference of up- vs down-momentum.
 8. DIRECTIONAL INDICATORS (plus_di/minus_di): up- and down-momentum
    measured separately; the raw ingredients of ADX.
 9. AROON (up/down/oscillator): bars since the window's max (up) / min
    (down) rate; oscillator = their difference.  "Time since extreme."
10. SUPERTREND (supertrend/_direction): volatility band above/below the
    rate; crossings flip the trend call.
11. ICHIMOKU CLOUD (tenkan/kijun/senkou_a/senkou_b): moving-average
    midpoints over 9/26/52-bar windows; slow multi-horizon trend framework.
    (5th line, chikou, duplicates the raw rate in our code -- excluded.)
12. LINEAR REGRESSION (linreg_slope/intercept): least-squares line fit over
    the window; slope = fitted trend, intercept = its level.
13. TRIX (triple_exp_rate_of_change): percent rate-of-change of a triple-
    smoothed EMA -- heavily filtered momentum.
14. VERTICAL HORIZONTAL FILTER (VHF): net movement / total path length --
    trending vs. choppy.
15. GOPALAKRISHNAN RANGE INDEX (GAPO): log(high-low range)/log(window
    length) -- VHF's sibling.
16. SYSTEM QUALITY NUMBER (SQN): mean rate-change / std of rate-changes --
    "trend quality."
17. INTER-ACCESS INTERVAL (inter_access_interval_ms, + variance): mean time
    gap between consecutive accesses (variance = gap irregularity).
    Memory-native (not from technical analysis).
18. RECENCY-WEIGHTED FREQUENCY: access rate with exponential decay favoring
    recent accesses.  Memory-native.
19. HEAT SCORE: the manager's own composite hotness estimate (blend of
    fault + PEBS sample counts, 0-1).  Memory-native; note it is also what
    the CURRENT heuristic thresholds.

FINAL CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. The synthetic and real-workload rankings PARTIALLY DISAGREE, and the
   disagreement has a known cause: the real-workload run's signal clock was
   stretched ~30x (159K tracked pages vs. a ~20-50K budget), which favors
   slow smoothed signals.  The single most important next experiment is
   rerunning GUPS with bounded tracking at a true 50ms cadence -- it decides
   whether Group 1 keeps the smoothers (linreg/ichimoku/supertrend) or
   reverts toward the volatility family (swing/RAVI).

2. Settled regardless of that question: RANDOM WALK INDEX is top-tier under
   every condition tested -- the one unconditional keeper.  SQN, TRIX, VHF,
   and GAPO are dead or directionless under every valid measurement -- the
   four unconditional drops.

3. ADX is the real-workload standout (perfect 1.00 consistency on both
   event types) and the biggest open verification item, since it was
   erratic at fine cadence.

4. The inter-access interval is the study's conditional asset: last place
   on collected real-workload data, but the only signal that ever LED a
   transition, with a concrete engineering path to restore it (timestamp
   PEBS samples at ingest).

5. Platform constraints for future collection: Haswell-class PEBS
   attributes stores only (loads report a garbage constant address;
   confirmed with stock perf tooling) -- full load+store telemetry needs
   Skylake+ (PageRank/Twitter queued on that).  Signal discrimination
   additionally requires holding the 50ms cadence, i.e. bounding tracked
   pages.
