SIGNAL GLOSSARY (what each of the 19 signals measures)
================================================================================

 1. RANDOM WALK INDEX (rwi_high/rwi_low): how far the access rate actually
    traveled over the window vs. how far a random walk would travel.  High =
    directional movement, not jitter.  Long- and short-window variants.
 2. SWING INDEX (swing_index): how decisive the current bar's move is --
    bar-to-bar rate change scaled by the bar's range.
 3. ACCUMULATIVE SWING INDEX (accum_swing_index): running cumulative sum of
    the swing index -- move size with memory.
 4. RANGE ACTION VERIFICATION INDEX (RAVI): percent gap between a short and
    a long moving average of the rate -- momentum vs. established trend.
 5. PARABOLIC SAR (parabolic_sar/_direction): trailing level that chases the
    rate and accelerates as a trend extends; direction = +1/-1 side flag.
 6. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE (schaff_trend_cycle/_signal): cycle detector on
    double-smoothed moving averages; 0-100, extremes = trend established.
    Needs several bars of evidence -- structurally a lagging confirmer.
 7. AVERAGE DIRECTIONAL INDEX (ADX): direction-free trend strength --
    smoothed, normalized difference of up- vs down-momentum.
 8. DIRECTIONAL INDICATORS (plus_di/minus_di): up- and down-momentum
    measured separately; the raw ingredients of ADX.
 9. AROON (up/down/oscillator): bars since the window's max (up) / min
    (down) rate; oscillator = their difference.  "Time since extreme."
10. SUPERTREND (supertrend/_direction): volatility band above/below the
    rate; crossings flip the trend call.
11. ICHIMOKU CLOUD (tenkan/kijun/senkou_a/senkou_b): moving-average
    midpoints over 9/26/52-bar windows; slow multi-horizon trend framework.
    (5th line, chikou, duplicates the raw rate in our code -- excluded.)
12. LINEAR REGRESSION (linreg_slope/intercept): least-squares line fit over
    the window; slope = fitted trend, intercept = its level.
13. TRIX (triple_exp_rate_of_change): percent rate-of-change of a triple-
    smoothed EMA -- heavily filtered momentum.
14. VERTICAL HORIZONTAL FILTER (VHF): net movement / total path length --
    trending vs. choppy.
15. GOPALAKRISHNAN RANGE INDEX (GAPO): log(high-low range)/log(window
    length) -- VHF's sibling.
16. SYSTEM QUALITY NUMBER (SQN): mean rate-change / std of rate-changes --
    "trend quality."
17. INTER-ACCESS INTERVAL (inter_access_interval_ms, + variance): mean time
    gap between consecutive accesses (variance = gap irregularity).
    Memory-native (not from technical analysis).
18. RECENCY-WEIGHTED FREQUENCY: access rate with exponential decay favoring
    recent accesses.  Memory-native.
19. HEAT SCORE: the manager's own composite hotness estimate (blend of
    fault + PEBS sample counts, 0-1).  Memory-native; note it is also what
    the CURRENT heuristic thresholds.

  worth stating because it bounds where the approach applies.  A first run
  (-n 2) produced no events at all; a 27x longer rerun (-n 10, 2.9M rows,

  window to score.  Root cause: the MEDIAN per-page access rate is 0.0.

  fix it (they admit more pages at the same thin spread).  It would need
  either a smaller graph (concentrating activity onto fewer pages) or a
  shorter PEBS sampling period.  The same arithmetic is why the lab
  harness's 8GB-hot-set GUPS config is unsuitable for per-page signals.

FINAL CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. RESOLVED: the bounded-tracking rerun (x2 replication, 741-765 events
   per type per run) settled the synthetic-vs-real disagreement.  The
   volatility family (random walk index, swing index) tops the real
   workload exactly as it topped the synthetics.  The coarse-clock
   champions collapsed on cue: ADX fell from 1.00/1.00 consistency to
   0.57, linear regression from #1 to a weak lagger -- both confirmed as
   cadence artifacts.  The synthetic-workload signal hierarchy GENERALIZES to real
   workloads.

2. Replication is excellent: r1/r2 agree to within a few percent on every
   headline number (rwi_high 2.12 vs 2.09; rwi_low on skewed 3.59 vs 3.57,
   both at 1.00 consistency).  The "single run" limitation is closed.

3. The leading signal is restored and characterized.  With hardware
   timestamps at ingest, inter_access_interval leads cold_to_hot by
   ~-450ms on the control (4.3 sigma).  On GUPS it is flat -- and so is
   every other signal, because GUPS relocates its hotset instantaneously:
   step transitions have no precursor for ANY signal to detect.  Lesson
   for phase-2: predictive lead exists where workloads warm up gradually;
   benchmarks with step transitions measure only reaction, not prediction.

4. Cadence honesty: rerun bars measured 143-205ms (~3-4x nominal) --
   labeled per dataset by the analysis' built-in cadence check.  A large
   improvement over the superseded ~30x run and sufficient for fast-vs-
   slow discrimination (demonstrated by the smoothers' collapse), with the
   residual stretch attributable to the PEBS record-table walk (a known,
   bounded engineering item if true 50ms is ever needed).

5. Platform constraints CONFIRMED BY FIX: on Ice Lake (r650), load
   attribution works (20,936 distinct pages in the smoke test vs 35-64 on
   Haswell), unblocking PageRank/Twitter -- which then reproduced the
   volatility-family hierarchy on load-driven telemetry (see Ranking B
   addendum).  Both mentor-provided workloads are now complete.  One new
   platform lesson from the fix: the load-latency facility (the Haswell
   workaround event) samples ~600x fewer loads than ALL_LOADS at the same
   period -- on fast machines that starves collection entirely; use
   ALL_LOADS (0x81d0) on Skylake+.
