LEAD/LAG ON REAL WORKLOADS
================================================================================
No signal leads a cold_to_hot transition on any real workload -- every top-5
signal moves at t=0 or after.

On hot_to_cold, swing_index shows a negative lead on 6 of the 8 datasets.  The
values are uniform at exactly -2 bars once the measured cadence is divided out
(BC -194ms, xsbench -372, hotset_move -400/-410, liblinear -457, PageRank -714;
bar spacing 97/186/200/205/228/357ms respectively).  That uniformity across
workloads with 3.7x different cadences is the signature of an analysis effect,
not a workload one: event detection median-smooths the rate before thresholding,
which places the event marker later than the raw rate change that swing_index
reacts to.  Treat it as an artifact of the detector unless a run with --smooth 1
reproduces it.

Leads reported as exactly -5 bars are the window edge -- "no clean onset found",
not a lead of that size.  The per-workload files print those as n/a.
