SIGNAL GLOSSARY (what each metric measures, in access-rate terms)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Every signal is computed per page over a rolling window of access-rate bars
(one bar per sampling interval; nominally 50ms -- ACTUAL bar width is
measured from timestamps and reported per dataset below).

  random_walk_index_high   How far the access rate actually moved over N bars
                           vs. what a random walk would produce.  High value =
                           trending, not noise.
  random_walk_index_low    Same, over a shorter lookback.
  swing_index              Size of the current bar's rate move relative to the
                           bar's total range ("how decisive was this step").
  accum_swing_index        Running cumulative sum of swing_index.
  schaff_trend_cycle       Cycle detector on double-smoothed moving averages;
                           0-100 oscillator.  Confirms trends; inherently lags.
  parabolic_sar            Trailing stop level that accelerates with a trend.
  parabolic_sar_direction  +1/-1: which side of the SAR the rate is on.
  range_action_verification_index (RAVI)
                           Short moving average vs. long moving average, in
                           percent; momentum vs. trend.
  avg_directional_index    Trend strength regardless of direction.
  aroon_down / aroon_up    Bars since the window's minimum / maximum rate.
  interval_access_rate     The raw per-bar access rate itself (not an
                           indicator; the underlying series).
  inter_access_interval_ms Mean time gap between consecutive accesses --
                           since this round, measured from HARDWARE PEBS
                           sample timestamps (EWMA of inter-sample gaps).
  system_quality_number    Mean rate-change divided by std of rate-changes.
  ichimoku_* family        Moving-average midpoints over 9/26/52-bar windows.
                           NOTE: ichimoku_chikou duplicates interval_access_
                           rate in our implementation -- excluded from ranks.
  linear_reg_slope /       Least-squares line fit over the lookback window.
    intercept
  recency_weighted_frequency  Access rate with exponential decay favoring
                           recent accesses.

WHAT WAS RUN
------------
All under telemetry-only collection: LD_PRELOAD shim intercepts the
application's large allocations; pages fault natively (zero measurable
overhead); access data comes exclusively from PEBS hardware sampling on
per-CPU events covering all threads; bounded page tracking
(LDOS_MIN_SAMPLES_TO_TRACK=3) holds the signal cadence; PEBS sample
timestamps drive the inter-access signal.

  A. hot_cold synthetic CONTROL (c220g2 Haswell, store-attributed)
     410 hot pages, 50K rows.  Bars measured 90ms (1.8x nominal).
     Events: 416 hot_to_cold, 71 cold_to_hot (8 windowed).

  B. GUPS hotset-move x2  (gups_hemem / HeMem benchmark; c220g2)
     90/10 skewed updates over a 2GB table; hot set RELOCATES mid-run.
     ~220K rows, ~3,000 pages per run.  Bars measured 200/205ms (4x).
     Events per run: ~790 cold_to_hot + ~785 hot_to_cold (741-765 windowed).
     REPLICATED: r1 and r2 agree within a few percent on every number below.

  C. GUPS skewed x2  (static 90/10 hot set; c220g2)
     ~74K rows, ~950-970 pages per run.  Bars measured 143ms (2.9x).
     Events per run: 128/129 hot_to_cold (96 windowed).  REPLICATED.

  D. GAPBS PAGERANK on the TWITTER GRAPH  (r650 Ice Lake Platinum 8360Y)
     61.5M nodes / 1.47B edges, 10 trials.  784K rows, 10,999 pages tracked
     VIA LOAD ATTRIBUTION -- the read-dominant workload class, previously
     impossible on Haswell-generation nodes (see HARDWARE FINDINGS).
     Bars measured 357ms (7.1x).  Events: 9,227 cold_to_hot + 9,005
     hot_to_cold (7,809 / 7,562 windowed) -- the study's largest event set.

================================================================================
CROSS-DATASET TAKEAWAYS (this round only)
================================================================================

1. RANDOM WALK INDEX is #1 or #2 in every dataset above -- on store-driven
   random-access updates (GUPS) AND load-driven power-law graph streaming
   (Twitter), replicated where runs were repeated.  swing_index is the
   consistent runner-up.

2. schaff_trend_cycle reacts in every dataset and lags in every dataset
   (+1-2 bars).  A confirmation signal, universally.

3. The leading signal (inter_access_interval_ms) works under production
   collection when transitions have warm-up ramps (control: -450ms lead)
   and cannot work when they are step functions (GUPS) -- nor does
   anything else.

4. ADX, linear regression, ichimoku, supertrend: mid-field or dead in
   every dataset at valid cadence.  Their earlier prominence was a
   measurement artifact of a 30x-stretched clock (superseded data).

================================================================================
HARDWARE FINDINGS (platform requirements for this telemetry)
================================================================================

1. Haswell/Broadwell (CloudLab c220g2/g5): PEBS LOAD data addresses are a
   garbage constant (0xfeb8xxxx) -- verified with stock perf mem.  STORE
   addresses are correct.  These nodes support store-driven telemetry only;
   read-only regions are invisible.

2. Ice Lake (CloudLab r650, Platinum 8360Y): load attribution verified
   correct -- smoke test sampled 20,936 distinct pages with reads across
   real heap addresses and no garbage constant.  This is what unblocked
   PageRank/Twitter.

3. New this round: the load-latency facility (event 0x1CD, used as the
   Haswell workaround) samples TAGGED loads only -- roughly 600x fewer
   samples than ALL_LOADS at the same period.  On the fast Ice Lake node
   it starved a 3.4s PageRank trial to ~500 read samples (empty dataset).
   Fixed by returning to ALL_LOADS (0x81d0), which is address-correct on
   Skylake+.  Lesson: sampling EVENT choice interacts with machine speed.

================================================================================
LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

1. Bar cadence ran 1.8-7.1x nominal (measured and labeled per dataset by
   the analysis' built-in cadence check; driver is the PEBS record-table
   walk at high page counts).  Sufficient for the rankings above -- the
   coarse-clock failure mode (smoothers taking over) demonstrably did not
   occur -- but lead/lag values are quantized to the measured bar width.
2. The control's cold_to_hot sample is small (n=8): unwritten pages are
   invisible until first attribution on store-only hardware.
3. Twitter collected on different hardware (Ice Lake) than the GUPS runs
   (Haswell) -- a necessity, not a choice; the signal hierarchy agreeing
   across both mitigates the concern.
4. GUPS runs replicated x2; Twitter and the control are single runs.
