CONFIGURATION DELTAS vs THE LAB'S run_workload.sh (ARMS harness)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
For comparability discussions: the lab harness runs GUPS at 16 threads /
2^36=64GB table / 2^33=8GB hot set, and PageRank at -i 150 -t 0 with
OMP_NUM_THREADS=16 + numactl -N0, interpose threshold 128MB, under DRAM
capacity budgets.  Our configs are smaller ON PURPOSE: per-page 50ms signal
series require PEBS sample density -- at an 8GB hot set (~2M hot pages),
each hot page is sampled roughly once a minute at our sampling period,
making per-page bars statistically empty.  Scale vs. per-page granularity
is a real trade-off to decide for phase 2.  (Our runs also apply no DRAM
budget: telemetry mode measures signals without capacity pressure.)
